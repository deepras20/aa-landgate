10 Things to Check Before Buying a Flat in Mumbai

Buying a flat in Mumbai is one of the largest financial decisions of your life. Beyond the excitement of finding the right home, due diligence is everything. Here are 10 non-negotiable checks our advisors conduct before recommending any property.

01
RERA Registration

Verify the project is registered on MahaRERA (maharera.mahaonline.gov.in). The RERA number should be visible on all marketing materials and the builder's website. An unregistered project has no legal protection for buyers.

02
Title & Ownership Verification

Commission an independent title search for at least 30 years. The property should have a clear, marketable title free of encumbrances, litigation, or disputed inheritance. Insist on seeing original documents, not photocopies.

03
Occupancy Certificate (OC)

The OC is issued by BMC/municipal authority confirming that construction is complete and the building is safe for occupation. Moving into a property without OC is illegal and makes obtaining utilities connections difficult.

04
Carpet Area vs Super Built-Up

Under RERA, builders must quote prices on carpet area. Understand what percentage is common area loading and ensure the carpet area matches the agreement figure. Mumbai builders often quote 30–40% loading — always negotiate on carpet area price.

05
Builder Track Record

Research the developer's history of on-time delivery, quality of construction, and handling of disputes. Check their MahaRERA complaint record. A builder with multiple delayed projects is a significant red flag.

+5

Additional checks include: approved building plan, encumbrance certificate, society formation status, amenities as promised, loan approval from reputable banks, and stamp duty / registration cost calculation. Our advisors conduct all 10 checks before any recommendation.
