Understanding Stamp Duty and Registration Charges in Maharashtra

The total acquisition cost of a property in Maharashtra includes more than just the price tag. Stamp duty and registration charges can add 6–7% to your transaction value — making them critical to budget for upfront.

Current Stamp Duty Rates (Maharashtra 2025)

CategoryStamp DutyRegistration
Mumbai Municipal Area5% of market value1% (max ₹30,000)
Rest of Maharashtra6% of market value1% (max ₹30,000)
Women Buyer (sole owner)4% (1% rebate)1%
Under-construction property5% + GST 5%1%

Example: ₹2 Crore Flat in Mumbai

Property Value₹2,00,00,000
Stamp Duty (5%)₹10,00,000
Registration (1%, capped)₹30,000
Total Acquisition Cost₹2,10,30,000

Note: For under-construction properties, GST at 5% (for properties above ₹45 lakh) applies on the property value, adding significantly to the total cost. Always factor this in before budgeting.
