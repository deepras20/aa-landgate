Mumbai Real Estate Prices:
Q1 2025 Report

Mumbai's residential property market posted a robust 11.4% year-on-year price growth in Q1 2025, driven by renewed buyer confidence, infrastructure completions, and a sustained surge in luxury housing demand.

Key Findings

Bandra West and Juhu continued to command the highest per-sqft rates in the city, averaging ₹45,000–₹60,000 per sqft for sea-facing apartments. The western suburbs from Andheri to Virar saw moderate growth of 8–12%, while Thane and Navi Mumbai recorded the strongest volume-based growth at 15–18% year-on-year as infrastructure projects matured.

The coastal belt — particularly Alibaug and Revdanda — saw extraordinary demand for villa and bungalow properties, with prices appreciating 22–28% as Mumbai's affluent class sought second-home options within 90 minutes of the city.

Home Buying · Apr 2025 · 7 min read

10 Things to Check Before
Buying a Flat in Mumbai

Buying a flat in Mumbai is one of the largest financial decisions of your life. Beyond the excitement of finding the right home, due diligence is everything. Here are 10 non-negotiable checks our advisors conduct before recommending any property.

01
RERA Registration

Verify the project is registered on MahaRERA (maharera.mahaonline.gov.in). The RERA number should be visible on all marketing materials and the builder's website. An unregistered project has no legal protection for buyers.

02
Title & Ownership Verification

Commission an independent title search for at least 30 years. The property should have a clear, marketable title free of encumbrances, litigation, or disputed inheritance. Insist on seeing original documents, not photocopies.

03
Occupancy Certificate (OC)

The OC is issued by BMC/municipal authority confirming that construction is complete and the building is safe for occupation. Moving into a property without OC is illegal and makes obtaining utilities connections difficult.

04
Carpet Area vs Super Built-Up

Under RERA, builders must quote prices on carpet area. Understand what percentage is common area loading and ensure the carpet area matches the agreement figure. Mumbai builders often quote 30–40% loading — always negotiate on carpet area price.

05
Builder Track Record

Research the developer's history of on-time delivery, quality of construction, and handling of disputes. Check their MahaRERA complaint record. A builder with multiple delayed projects is a significant red flag.

+5

Additional checks include: approved building plan, encumbrance certificate, society formation status, amenities as promised, loan approval from reputable banks, and stamp duty / registration cost calculation. Our advisors conduct all 10 checks before any recommendation.

Is 2025 a Good Time to
Invest in Mumbai Property?

Short answer: yes — but with caveats. 2025 presents a rare convergence of favourable factors for property investment in Maharashtra, though smart timing and location selection remain critical.

Why 2025 Is a Strong Entry Year

Interest Rate Stability: The RBI held the repo rate at 6.5% through most of 2024–25, keeping home loan rates predictable. Most buyers can lock in rates between 8.4–8.9% — historically favourable compared to the 10–12% rates of the 2010s.

Metro Connectivity Boost: Mumbai Metro Lines 2A, 7, and 9 have opened up corridors from Andheri to Dahisar and from Versova to Borivali, dramatically cutting commute times and boosting property values in previously underserved areas.

Post-COVID Housing Upgrade: Work-from-home culture has driven sustained demand for larger homes. Buyers who downgraded during 2020–22 are now actively upgrading, sustaining volume at the 2–5 Cr price band.

NRI Demand Surge: With the Indian rupee depreciation making property more attractive in dollar/dirham terms, NRI investment in Mumbai real estate hit a 10-year high in FY25, with particular concentration in Bandra, Juhu, and Alibaug.

NRI Guide · Jan 2025 · 8 min read

Complete NRI Guide to
Buying Property in India

Non-Resident Indians represent one of the most significant buyer segments in Mumbai's premium property market. With over $30 billion in annual remittances, NRIs have deep capacity — but navigating FEMA regulations, banking requirements, and documentation from abroad requires expert guidance.

Can NRIs Buy Property in India?

Yes. Under FEMA (Foreign Exchange Management Act), NRIs and PIOs (Persons of Indian Origin) can freely purchase residential and commercial properties in India. There is no limit on the number of properties you can own, and no RBI permission is required for most transactions.

Exception: Agricultural land, plantation property, and farmhouses cannot be purchased by NRIs without specific RBI approval.

Payment & Banking Rules

  • Payments must be made through NRE or NRO accounts held in India
  • Foreign currency can be remitted through banking channels — no cash transactions
  • Home loans can be availed from Indian banks for NRI purchases
  • EMI payments must be made from NRE/NRO accounts or through inward remittances

Repatriation of Sale Proceeds

On sale, NRIs can repatriate up to the original investment amount (in foreign currency equivalent). Capital gains can also be repatriated, subject to applicable taxes. For two properties purchased from NRE account funds, full repatriation of the principal is permitted without RBI approval.

AI & Technology · Dec 2024 · 5 min read

How AI Is Transforming
Real Estate Valuation in India

For decades, property valuation in India was an opaque art — driven by builder-quoted prices, hearsay, and gut instinct. AI is changing that permanently, and A&A Landgate is at the forefront of applying this technology in Maharashtra.

The Traditional Problem

India's property market suffers from endemic information asymmetry. Sellers have access to the full sales history of their building; buyers typically do not. Brokers often have a vested interest in a particular price direction. Government circle rates frequently lag true market values by 2–5 years.

The result: buyers routinely overpay by 5–15% in premium markets, and sellers underprice by similar margins in slower markets. The information gap cost Indian property buyers an estimated ₹4,200 crore in avoidable overpayments in FY2024.

How AI Valuation Works

Modern AI valuation engines trained on Maharashtra property registration data, Circle Rate histories, infrastructure project timelines, and micro-market demographic shifts can now produce price estimates within 3–5% of actual transaction prices — comparable to the best international AVM (Automated Valuation Model) systems.

Key inputs include: registered transaction data (IGR Maharashtra), floor, view orientation, building age, lift availability, car parking status, proximity to metro stations, and school/hospital micro-scores.

A&A Landgate's Predictive Valuation Tool

Our proprietary model processes over 18 data variables per property and generates a 12-month forward price estimate alongside a current fair value range. Clients using our valuation tool have saved an average of ₹8–22 lakh per transaction by avoiding overpayment — and sellers have achieved 4–9% higher sale prices by correct positioning.
